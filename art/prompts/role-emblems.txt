A set of 14 simple circular emblem icons for character roles in a folk-horror
fishing-town game. Each emblem is a single bold symbol inside a circular
rope-bordered frame. High contrast, readable at small size.
Symbols: a lit lantern, a magnifying glass, a medical cross made of bandages,
a constable's whistle, a town seal, a spiral seashell, a ship's wheel, an oil can,
a hooded figure, a shifting mask, crossed knives, a silenced bell, an empty coat,
a fishing hook.
Arranged in a grid on a flat background, evenly spaced, no text.
